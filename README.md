# StudyZen AI Teacher

Rebuild and upgrade the existing StudyZen app into a REAL interactive AI classroom.

IMPORTANT:

This is NOT a normal chatbot and NOT a generic educational website.

StudyZen must feel like having a personal human-like teacher who teaches a student exactly like a good college/school teacher would teach in a real classroom.

Use the uploaded StudyZen logo image as the official app logo.

Do NOT redesign, simplify, or replace the uploaded logo.

5. NO SUBJECT SELECTION

==================================================

DO NOT show a subject selection dropdown inside the AI Teacher.

The student should be able to ask ANY question.

The AI must automatically detect:

- Subject

- Topic

- Level/context when possible

Examples:

"What is photosynthesis?"

→ Biology/Science

"What is data mining?"

→ Computer Science

"What is Newton's third law?"

→ Physics

"What is supply and demand?"

→ Economics

Show the detected topic as a small informational label if useful, but NEVER require the student to choose a subject.

==================================================

6. AI TEACHER — MOST IMPORTANT FEATURE

==================================================

The AI Teacher must NOT behave like ChatGPT simply displaying a long answer.

DO NOT make the student read a large paragraph and then click "Listen".

The teacher must TEACH.

The experience should feel like a real teacher explaining in class.

Example:

Student asks:

"What is a supernova?"

The AI Teacher should start speaking naturally:

"Okay, let's understand what a supernova actually is. First, imagine a very massive star..."

Then explain the concept step by step.

The teacher should naturally combine:

1. Simple theory

2. Real-life analogy

3. Practical explanation

4. Example

5. Important points

6. Small question for the student

7. Student response

8. Teacher reaction

9. Continue the lesson

The teacher should NOT dump the complete answer at once.

==================================================

7. VOICE-FIRST TEACHING

==================================================

This is extremely important.

The AI must NOT simply display an answer and provide a "Listen" button that reads the existing paragraph.

Instead:

AI Teacher should actively speak the explanation as a teacher.

Show a teacher visual/avatar while the AI is speaking.

When AI is speaking:

- Show "Teacher is speaking..."

- Animate the teacher naturally where possible

- Show a subtle voice waveform/audio indicator

- Board updates along with the teaching

When the student speaks:

- Microphone activates

- Capture student's speech

- Convert it to text internally

- Understand the student's response

- Teacher responds naturally

The goal is:

Teacher speaks

↓

Student listens

↓

Teacher asks a question

↓

Student speaks

↓

Teacher understands

↓

Teacher responds

↓

Lesson continues

Do NOT make voice interaction feel like text-to-speech reading.

If a real talking-avatar/lip-sync service is not configured, create the best available teacher-speaking animation and voice-first architecture without pretending that a static image is truly lip-synced.

Keep the architecture ready for a real avatar/lip-sync API later.

==================================================

Do NOT make the teacher just a tiny icon beside chat messages.

The teacher should be a major part of the classroom interface.

==================================================

10. TEACHING BEHAVIOR

==================================================

The AI should teach one concept at a time.

Do NOT give unnecessarily long explanations.

Use simple language.

Avoid book-like wording.

Explain as a real teacher would.

Use phrases naturally such as:

- "Let's understand this first."

- "Think of it like this..."

- "Now tell me..."

- "Exactly!"

- "You're close."

- "Let's try another example."

- "No problem, I'll explain it another way."

Do not overuse the same phrases.

==================================================

11. STUDENT INTERACTION

==================================================

The teacher must interact with the student during the lesson.

Ask small questions at appropriate points.

Example:

Teacher:

"Now, what do you think happens when the star runs out of fuel?"

Student:

"I think it explodes."

Teacher:

"Exactly! You're on the right track. Now let's see why that happens."

The teacher should understand the student's answer and continue accordingly.

Do not interrupt every few seconds.

Use interaction at meaningful points.

==================================================

12. WHEN STUDENT DOESN'T UNDERSTAND

==================================================

If student says:

"I don't understand"

"Explain again"

"I didn't get it"

"Can you explain simply?"

The AI MUST NOT repeat the same answer.

Instead:

- acknowledge the student

- simplify the concept

- use a DIFFERENT analogy

- use a DIFFERENT example

- explain visually when possible

- ask a smaller checking question

Example:

First explanation:

"Supernova is the explosion of a massive star."

If student doesn't understand:

"Okay, forget the scientific definition for a moment. Imagine a huge balloon that has been under pressure..."

Use a different explanation.

The teacher should remember what was already explained during the current lesson.

==================================================

14. DIGITAL BLACKBOARD

==================================================

Create a large classroom-style BLACK DIGITAL BOARD.

The board is NOT just a text box.

- Topic title

- Definition

- Important points

- Keywords

- Formulas

- Step-by-step processes

- Examples

- Flowcharts

- Diagrams

- Simple visual explanations

The board should reveal information progressively as the teacher teaches.

Do NOT show the entire final answer immediately.

SUPERNOVA

     ↓

     ↓

     ↓

==================================================

↓

Do not dump the complete board at the beginning.

Make it feel like the teacher is actually writing/teaching on the board.

==================================================

17. PRACTICAL EXPLANATION

==================================================

Whenever appropriate, explain concepts practically.

Do not only give definitions.

Examples:

Physics:

connect the concept to everyday movement.

Computer Science:

connect concepts to apps/websites students use.

Database:

use a college/student database example.

AI:

use familiar applications.

Economics:

use shopping/market examples.

Mathematics:

use real-world calculations.

Science:

use everyday observations.

The teacher should always try to answer:

"Where do I see this in real life?"
